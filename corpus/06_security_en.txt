CloudBox Security Overview (EN)

Encryption at rest: all files are encrypted with AES-256. Encryption keys are rotated every 90 days and stored in a dedicated key management service.
Encryption in transit: TLS 1.3 is required for every client connection; older protocols are rejected.

Access control: two-factor authentication (2FA) is available on every plan and is mandatory for Team administrators. Single sign-on (SSO) via SAML is included in the Team plan only.

Compliance: CloudBox completed a SOC 2 Type II audit in 2025. Data processing follows GDPR; a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) can be signed on request.

Incident response: customers are notified about a confirmed security incident within 72 hours by email.
